estudio-canais

estudio-canais is a private, single-user tool that reads performance metrics (views, watch time, retention, impressions, click-through rate and estimated revenue) from YouTube channels owned and operated by its developer, using the official YouTube Data, Analytics and Reporting APIs.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users. Access is read-only: the tool does not upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube through these APIs.
